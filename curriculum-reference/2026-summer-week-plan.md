## Teaching style
Every day is **project-based learning**: a real-world story hook → a driving question /
mission → just-enough concept teaching → a build challenge with clear success criteria →
test-and-improve (engineering design process) → reflect & share. Lesson decks behind each
day live in `lessons.json` and are presented via the Live Lesson tool.
