Concept

Race Engineering Tradeoffs

Speed

Faster cars can finish sooner, but mistakes cost time.

Accuracy

Good steering keeps the car inside the path.

Reliability

A winning design survives repeated runs without falling apart.

LEGO Building Tips

Technic Tips: Race Ready Cars

Reinforce Steering

Race turns stress the linkage, so brace loose joints.

Stay Low

A low build is less likely to tip during turns.

Protect the Front

Bumpers and sensors should survive cone taps.

Builder advice: Run a slow clean lap before trying to go faster.
Build Check

Is It Course Ready?

Turns Cleanly

The car can make the course turns without jamming.

Stays Together

Steering and bumpers stay attached after multiple runs.

Tracks Straight

Between turns, the car does not drift too much.

The BioGlow team needs a rover that can deliver glowing samples through a winding habitat route. Engineers must finish quickly without leaving the safe path.

Mission prompt: Where should your rover slow down, and where can it safely speed up?
Challenge

Challenge Rules: Steering Race

1Build a steering car that can complete a marked course.
2Start behind the line with the car facing forward.
3Complete the route as cleanly as possible.
4Score by finish, accuracy, and time.
5Add penalties for cones hit or leaving the path.
6Run three official trials after practice.
Fair Testing

Keep the Race Fair

Same Course

Use the same cones, turns, and lane width.

Same Timing

Start and stop the timer the same way.

Same Penalties

Agree on penalties before official runs.

Improve

Improve Your Race Run

Try Changing

  • Steering angle
  • Speed setting
  • Wheel grip
  • Chassis width

Watch For

  • Wide turns
  • Oversteering
  • Loose pins
  • Top-heavy tipping
Reflection

After Testing: Reflection

1. What was the best balance of speed and control?
2. Which part of the course caused the most trouble?
3. What design change improved reliability?
4. What would you carry into a final robot design?
