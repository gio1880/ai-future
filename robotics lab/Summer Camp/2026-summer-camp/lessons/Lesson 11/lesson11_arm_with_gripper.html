Arm with Gripper

Mechanisms & Motion challenge: combine reach, lift, and grip into one controlled build.

Quick Review

From Gripping to Reaching

Grip Force

The gripper needed enough friction to hold an object.

Pivot Points

Jaws moved around pivots to open and close.

Control

Today the mechanism must grip and place at a distance.

Today's Goal

Lesson Goal

Today I will combine lift, grip, leverage, reach, stability, and payload control to build an arm with a gripper.

Concept

What Changes When the Gripper Gets an Arm?

Leverage

A longer arm can reach farther but is harder to support.

Stability

The base must resist tipping when the arm reaches out.

Payload

The object adds weight at the end of the arm.

LEGO Building Tips

Technic Tips: Arms with Grippers

Brace the Arm

Use paired beams or triangles so the arm does not bend.

Widen the Base

A wide, heavy base helps stop tipping.

Keep Motion Clear

Make sure the gripper linkage does not rub the arm or tower.

Builder advice: Test the arm without the object first, then test again while holding the payload.
Build Check

Reach Without Tipping

Stable Base

The base should stay down when the arm reaches forward.

Strong Joint

The shoulder joint should not wobble or separate.

Clear Gripper

The gripper should still open and close at the end of the arm.

BioGlow Mission

BioGlow Mission: Place the Supply Crate

The BioGlow team needs a robotic arm to lift and place supply crates into a safe zone. Engineers must reach, grip, lift, and release without tipping the base.

Mission prompt: How can your arm reach farther while staying stable?
Challenge

Challenge Rules: Reach, Grab, Place

1Build an arm with a working gripper.
2Start with the object in the pickup zone.
3Grab the object without using your hand on the object.
4Lift or move the object to the target zone.
5Release the object inside the target zone.
6Record reach distance and success.
Fair Testing

Keep the Test Fair

Same Object

Use the same crate or classroom item.

Same Zones

Mark pickup and target zones before testing.

Same Attempts

Each team gets the same number of trials.

Improve

Improve Your Arm

Try Changing

  • Base width
  • Arm length
  • Joint bracing
  • Gripper angle

Watch For

  • Base tipping
  • Arm bending
  • Object slipping
  • Linkage rubbing
Reflection

After Testing: Reflection

1. What helped your arm reach farther?
2. What helped it stay stable?
3. What was harder: gripping, lifting, or placing?
4. What would you redesign next?
