Try It: Pull vs. Push

↔

Tension

Pull your hands apart.

The structure is being stretched.

→←

Compression

Push your hands together.

The structure is being squeezed.

Bridge connection: the top may get squeezed, while the bottom may get stretched.

Bridge Members in LEGO Language

What is a member?

A member is one piece of a structure.

  • Beam
  • Axle
  • Frame
  • Diagonal support

Ties & Struts

Tie = member being pulled

Strut = member being pushed

Ties pull. Struts push.

Improved Bridge Challenge Rules

1. Span a 14-inch gap.
2. Stand without being held.
3. Hold a cup in the center.
4. Hold as many marbles as possible.
5. Use at least two triangles.
6. Stay standing for 10 seconds.
Testing rule: once testing begins, hands must be off the bridge.
Added Slide

Fair Build Rules

Each team gets the same starting pieces.
Use only pieces from your team bin.
No taking pieces from another team.
Ask before trading pieces.
When the timer ends, hands go up.
