Concept

What Makes Steering Work?

Pivot

Steering wheels rotate around a pivot point.

Linkage

A linkage makes both front wheels turn together.

Turning Radius

A sharper angle makes a tighter turn, but can be harder to control.

LEGO Building Tips

Technic Tips: Steering

Mirror the Sides

Both steering sides should be the same length and angle.

Limit Oversteer

Too much angle can make wheels jam or drag sideways.

Brace the Front

Steering adds force to the front; support it with extra beams.

Builder advice: Push the car by hand first and make sure the wheels return close to straight.
Build Check

Can It Turn and Recover?

Left and Right

The car should turn both directions.

Nearly Straight

After a turn, wheels should return near center.

Fits the Space

The car should be smaller than the parking box.

Challenge

Challenge Rules: Self Parking

1Build a car with working steering.
2Create a parking box with tape or beams.
3Start from the same angle each trial.
4Drive or program the car into the box.
5Score accuracy, control, and no collisions.
6Try three official parking attempts.
Fair Testing

Keep the Test Fair

Same Box

Use the same parking size for every team trial.

Same Start

Keep the start line and angle the same.

Same Rules

Decide what counts as inside before testing.

Improve

Improve Parking Accuracy

Try Changing

  • Steering angle
  • Wheelbase length
  • Driving speed
  • Sensor trigger or timing

Watch For

  • Overshooting the box
  • Turning too late
  • Wheels jamming
  • Chassis twisting
Reflection

After Testing: Reflection
