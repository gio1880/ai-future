📥 Update lesson content

Pulls the newest lesson wording, questions, and videos from the latest deployment onto the live hub. Student progress, submitted answers, and any due dates you set are kept as they are. Nothing is deleted.

🔁 Convert old lessons

Lessons made with the old "Add assignment" form were copied once per student, so students who joined later never got them and each showed up as many separate rows. Converting turns each into a single lesson for the teams that had it. Every answer, grade and draft moves with it, and a backup is saved first. Preview before converting.
