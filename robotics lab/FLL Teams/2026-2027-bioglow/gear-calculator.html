⚙️ Gear Calculator

Work out what a gear train does to your motor before you build it. Pick your motor, add the gears in the order they mesh, and see whether you end up faster or stronger — and by how much.

1. Which motor?

The two SPIKE Prime motors your team has.

2. Your gear train

One row per pair of meshing gears. The driver is the gear the power comes from, the follower is the one it turns. If you have a second pair after that, add another stage.

How to read this

  • Small gear driving a big gear = geared down. Slower, stronger. Use it for lifting and pushing.
  • Big gear driving a small gear = geared up. Faster, weaker. Use it for spinning and sweeping.
  • You never get both. Every gear train is you choosing one.
  • Each extra pair of gears costs a little to friction, so use the fewest that get you the ratio you need.
