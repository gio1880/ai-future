📖 Pybricks commands that run here
robot.straight(500) — drive forward 500 mm (negative = reverse)
rotations(2) — drive 2 wheel rotations (standard wheel: 2 × 17.6 cm)
robot.turn(90) — turn right 90° (negative = left)
robot.drive(200, 30) — arc: 200 mm/s while turning 30°/s, until robot.stop()
wait(1000) — pause for 1000 ms
hub.speaker.beep() — play a beep & flash the robot
robot.settings(straight_speed=720) — set straight speed in mm/s
if hub.imu.ready(): — accepted; indented robot commands inside will run
