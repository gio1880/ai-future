# Local Habitat Ideas for BIOGLOW

Use this as a starting board for Innovation Project research.

## Places to Observe

- School garden, courtyard, or playground edges
- Street trees and tree pits near the school
- Local parks, ponds, wetlands, rivers, or shorelines
- Vacant lots, medians, and small green spaces
- Apartment planters, rooftops, and community gardens

## Problems to Investigate

- Pollinators have too few native plants or safe resting places.
- Trash changes how wildlife uses a park or street.
- Street trees struggle with heat, compacted soil, or poor watering.
- Birds are affected by glass, nighttime light, or noise.
- Invasive species crowd out native plants.
- Rainwater flooding, runoff, or erosion damages a local habitat.
- People do not know which plants or habits support biodiversity.

## Research Questions

- Which species use this place now?
- What does this habitat provide: food, water, shelter, nesting space, shade, or soil?
- What is missing or harming the ecosystem?
- Who is affected: students, residents, gardeners, park workers, animals, plants, or pollinators?
- What solution could we prototype, test, and improve before competition?
