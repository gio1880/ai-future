The big rules

Our 3 Camp Rules

Everything we do fits into these three. Be Safe. Be Kind. Be a Builder.

Rule 1

🦺 Be Safe

✓Walk in the classroom and keep hands to yourself.
✓Use tools and kits the right way — ask if you're not sure.
✓Keep small pieces out of mouths and off the floor.
Rule 2

💙 Be Kind

✓Listen when a coach or teammate is talking.
✓Share ideas, take turns, and cheer each other on.
✓Use kind words — everyone is learning.
Rule 3

🔧 Be a Builder

✓Keep your pieces in your team bin — don't trade without asking.
✓Try, test, and improve — mistakes help us learn.
✓Clean up and sort the kit when time is up.
