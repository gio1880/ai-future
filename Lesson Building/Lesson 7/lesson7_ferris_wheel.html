Concept

What Makes Rotation Smooth?

Axle Support

The axle needs support on both sides.

Balance

Even spacing keeps one side from pulling too hard.

Low Friction

Parts should turn freely without rubbing.

LEGO Building Tips

Technic Tips: Ferris Wheels

Support the Axle

Hold the center axle from both sides so it stays level while turning.

Match Opposite Sides

Build pods and spokes in pairs across from each other to keep balance.

Reinforce the Rim

Connect beams into triangles or braces so the wheel keeps its shape.

Builder advice: If the wheel always falls to one side, check whether one pod is heavier or farther from the center.
Build Check

Rigid Wheel

BioGlow Mission

BioGlow Mission: Observation Wheel

The BioGlow team needs a rotating observation wheel to scan the glowing habitat from different heights. Engineers must build a wheel that turns smoothly and carries balanced sensor pods.

Mission prompt: How can your team keep the wheel balanced while it rotates?
Challenge

Challenge Rules: Smooth Ferris Wheel

1Build a standing wheel with a central axle.
2Add at least 4 passenger or sensor pods.
3The wheel must rotate by hand, crank, or gear.
4The stand must stay upright during rotation.
5Pods should stay attached for one full turn.
6Score smoothness, balance, and reliability.
Fair Testing

Keep the Test Fair

Same Turn

Test one complete rotation.

Same Load

Use the same pod pieces or weights.

Same Score

Count stops, wobbles, and parts falling off.

Improve

Improve Your Ferris Wheel

Try Changing

  • Axle support width
  • Pod spacing
  • Wheel bracing
  • Crank or gear connection

Watch For

  • Wobbling
  • Uneven weight
  • Axle bending
  • Rubbing against the stand
Reflection

After Testing: Reflection

1. What helped the wheel rotate smoothly?
2. What made the wheel wobble or stop?
3. How did balance affect the motion?
4. What would you change next?
