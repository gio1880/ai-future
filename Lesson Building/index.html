Camp Flow

1
Build strong foundations.

Introduce Technic connections, structures, and stable bases.

2
Build across and upward.

Use bridges, towers, and lifts to explore strength, balance, and controlled motion.

3
Build motion systems.

Use cars, launches, rotating wheels, and marble runs to test energy and movement.

4
Build sensor-aware robots.

Add distance, color, and steering decisions so rovers can respond to a course.

5
Build like engineers.

Run fair tests, improve one feature at a time, and connect each challenge to the BioGlow mission.
