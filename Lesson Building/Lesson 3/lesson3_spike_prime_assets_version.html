New Piece Focus: Axles Make Motion

Pins Hold

Pins are great when you want beams and frames to stay connected.

They helped us build towers and bridges.

Axles Turn

Axles are great when you want wheels, gears, or arms to rotate together.

They help a car roll straight and smoothly.

Rule to remember: If the part needs to spin, check whether it needs an axle.

Build Check: Can Your Wheels Spin Freely?

Aligned Axle

The wheels sit on the same straight axle and can rotate smoothly.

Three Quick Tests

1Spin each wheel with your finger.
2Roll the car gently on the table.
3Look for rubbing, wobbling, or crooked axles.

The BioGlow team needs a small rover to carry samples through the habitat. It has no motor today, so engineers will power it with stored air from a balloon.

Smooth Axles

Challenge Rules: Balloon Powered Car

1The balloon is the only power source.
2The car must start behind the line.
3Hands off after release.
4Measure from the start line to where the car stops.
5Test at least two design changes.
6Record your best distance and fastest 1-foot time.
Added Slide

Improve Your Design

Reduce Friction

Check whether wheels rub against beams or connectors.

Keep It Straight

Line up axles so the car does not curve too much.

Control Air

Point the balloon nozzle backward so the car moves forward.

Iteration prompt: Change one thing, test again, and compare the result.
Added Slide

After Testing: Reflection

1. What design helped your car travel farther?
2. What slowed your car down?
3. Did your axles and wheels spin smoothly?
4. What would you change if you had 10 more minutes?
Teacher Slide

Quick Lesson Flow

Before Building

  • Review bridges, triangles, tension, and compression.
  • Introduce axles and axle connectors.
  • Explain how axles help wheels spin and stay aligned.
  • Connect balloon propulsion to Newton's Third Law.

During / After Building

  • Build the balloon powered car.
  • Check wheel spin and axle alignment.
  • Run distance and sprint tests.
  • Make one change at a time and retest.
  • Reflect on what improved performance.
