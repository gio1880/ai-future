BioGlow Mission: Launch the Glow Marker

The BioGlow team needs to send a glowing marker across the habitat to show the next safe work zone. Engineers must choose between launching far and landing accurately.

Distance

Can your launcher send the marker far enough to reach the zone?

Accuracy

Can your launcher hit the target instead of just flying far?

Mission prompt: What design change gives you more control without losing too much distance?

Challenge 1 Rules: Longest Launch

1Use the same projectile for every team.
2Launch from the same line.
3Measure from the line to where the projectile first lands.
4Each team gets 3 attempts.
5Record the best distance.
6Change one part and test again if time allows.
Original Slide 7Challenge 2: Target Accuracy
Added Slide

Challenge 2 Rules: Best Accuracy

1Place the target 6 inches from the catapult.
2Keep the catapult behind the launch line.
3Each team gets 3 shots.
4Adjust one design feature at a time.
5Record hits, misses, and near misses.
6The highest score wins the accuracy round.

After Testing: Reflection

1. What helped your catapult launch farther?
2. What helped your catapult aim more accurately?
3. Did a longer arm make the launcher better or harder to control?
4. What would you change if you had 10 more minutes?
Teacher Slide

Quick Lesson Flow

Before Building

  • Review stored energy from the balloon car.
  • Introduce elastic potential energy.
  • Explain fulcrum, effort, and load.
  • Review stability before launch power.

During / After Building

  • Build and reinforce the catapult.
  • Run distance trials.
  • Adjust for target accuracy.
  • Compare distance, accuracy, and control.
  • Reflect on the best design tradeoff.
