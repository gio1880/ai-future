Concept

What Makes a Car Roll Well?

Chassis

The chassis is the frame that holds everything together.

Wheelbase

The distance between front and back wheels affects stability.

Alignment

Parallel axles help the car roll straight.

LEGO Building Tips

Technic Tips: Simple Cars

Square the Frame

Use beams to make a rectangular chassis that does not twist.

Parallel Axles

Front and back axles should point the same direction.

Equal Wheel Spacing

Wheels should sit the same distance from the frame on both sides.

Builder advice: Roll the car by hand before adding extra pieces; fix wobble early.
Build Check

Roll Straight First

Free Wheels

Wheels should spin without rubbing the frame.

Strong Bumpers

Front and back pieces should not fall off during testing.

Flat Chassis

The frame should not drag on the floor.

BioGlow Mission: Scout Car

The BioGlow team needs a reliable scout car that can move smoothly through the habitat. Engineers must build a sturdy chassis that rolls straight and survives repeated trials.

Mission prompt: How can your car stay straight and stable while moving through the habitat?
Challenge

Challenge Rules: Reliable Simple Car

1Build a four-wheel car with a sturdy chassis.
2Start behind the line.
3Release or gently roll the car forward.
4The car should travel straight through the test lane.
5No pieces should fall off.
6Record distance, straightness, and reliability.
Fair Testing

Keep the Test Fair

Same Lane

Use the same path or tape lines.

Same Start

Place the car behind the same start line.

Same Push

Use the same release method for each trial.

Improve

Improve Your Car

Try Changing

  • Wheel spacing
  • Axle support
  • Frame bracing
  • Weight placement

Watch For

  • Pulling left or right
  • Wobbly wheels
  • Frame dragging
  • Loose bumpers
Reflection

After Testing: Reflection

1. What helped your car roll straight?
2. What caused wobble or turning?
3. Which connection needed the most reinforcement?
4. What would you change before adding a motor or sensor?
