## 5. Patterns across the highest-performing creative

After watching dozens of these the same five patterns repeat:

**Hook in 1.5 seconds, every time.** Best openers: a robot doing something surprising, a kid's reaction face, or the on-screen text "Most parents don't know this…" / "This is what $99 a week gets your kid."

**Native vertical video, not landscape.** Even iD Tech's Meta ads are now shot 9:16. Re-cropping a 16:9 video kills CTR.

**Kid voiceover beats parent voiceover.** The viral RoboLancers/FIRST clip works because actual teens are reacting. CrunchLabs leans on Mark Rober but the demos are kid-driven.

**Show the build, not the brochure.** Highest-engagement videos are 60–80% gameplay/build B-roll, 20–40% talking head. Brochure shots tank.

**One CTA, in text, on screen, the whole time.** "Spots filling for Week 3" or "Early-bird ends June 15" displayed persistently outperforms voiceover-only CTAs.

**Series, not one-shots.** CrunchLabs releases an episode per planet. FIRST does season reveals. Algorithms reward serialized accounts. We should run a "Week of…" series across our 9 weeks.

## 6. Recommended action plan for AI Future Meta ads

Phase 1 — Steal-with-pride (this week):
1. Open the Meta Ad Library links in section 1 above.
2. For each brand, screenshot every ad that's been running 30+ days. Save into `/swipe-file/`.
3. Rank top 10 by gut feel — those are your templates.

Phase 2 — Production (next 2 weeks):
1. Film 5 vertical 15-sec hooks at the West Chester campus: kid building a robot, robot doing something cool, kid reacting, parent dropping off, group cheer.
2. Cut into 3–5 ad variants using each Meta Ad Library template you swiped.
3. Always end with the exact CTA "Reserve Week ___ — early bird $450 until June 15."

Phase 3 — Launch (week of May 4):
1. Start with $20/day per ad set, 3 ad sets, 3 creatives each = $180/day.
2. Geo-target: Westchester County + 25-mi radius around White Plains.
3. Audience: Parents of children 6–14, interests = STEM, coding, FIRST Robotics, LEGO.
4. Kill any ad below 1.5% CTR after 48 hours. Scale anything above 3% CTR by 50% daily.
